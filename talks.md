# Talks

I speak regularly to the Cardano community in Sri Lanka, in English, Tamil and Sinhala. If you'd like me to speak at your meetup or event, [get in touch](/about#contact).

## 2025

### CoinCeylon x Intersect Sri Lanka Hub town halls

Monthly, January 2025 to present. Livestreamed on YouTube.

I present the monthly community town hall: what changed in the Cardano ecosystem, what governance proposals mean for ordinary holders, and what is coming on the roadmap. Each session ends with live questions from the community. More than ten sessions so far.

### Industry Expo Sri Lanka 2025

BMICH, Colombo. September 2025.

I represented Coinceylon at the CoinCeylon x Intersect Sri Lanka booth for all four days of the expo, which drew over 5,000 attendees. I spoke with more than 500 visitors about Cardano, its community and what Coinceylon is building.
